What this is. Every alert this bot produces is recorded and settled automatically against later price history, as if it had been taken. These are simulated results, not a record of real trades, and nothing here is financial advice.

Costs are deducted at 5 points per round turn. Bars that span both stop and target are counted as losses, since the order they were touched in is unknowable. Backtesting found this strategy has a small edge of roughly 0.08R per trade against typical costs nearer 0.2R — so a negative total here is the expected result, not a malfunction.
